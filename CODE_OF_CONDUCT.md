# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome good-faith questions, reproduce reported problems, and critique scientific arguments rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Represent evidence and uncertainty honestly. Do not fabricate credentials, citations, measurements or validation results. Credit prior work and respect data licenses.

The repository owner is responsible for moderation and should establish a private reporting contact before opening the project to contributions. Until that contact is provided, do not publish sensitive complaints or personal information in public issues. Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive contributions and restrict participation when necessary.
